# Results Paragraph Draft: AF-Mediated Effects

### 3.7 Mediation analysis

To explore whether AF may mediate part of the inflammatory protein-HF association, we first estimated the genetic effect of AF liability on HF using 73 harmonised AF instruments selected at P < 5 x 10^-8 and distance-pruned at 10 Mb. The fixed-effect IVW estimate supported a positive AF -> HF association (OR 1.2653, 95% CI 1.233-1.2984; P=3.57e-71).

For FGF5, the AF-mediated indirect effect had OR 1.0145 (95% CI 1.0093-1.0197; P=3.87e-08), corresponding to an exploratory mediated proportion of 49.7%. For LPA, the AF-mediated indirect effect had OR 1.0081 (95% CI 1.0022-1.014; P=0.0068), corresponding to an exploratory mediated proportion of 10.5%.

These estimates suggest that AF-mediated pathways may contribute to the observed FGF5/LPA-HF associations, but the analysis is exploratory because it uses summary-level binary outcome estimates, distance-pruned AF instruments without an LD reference panel, and product-of-coefficients assumptions. The mediation results were therefore used as supportive evidence in target prioritization rather than as definitive proof of a mechanistic AF-mediated pathway.
